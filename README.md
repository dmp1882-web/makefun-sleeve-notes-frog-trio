# Sleeve Notes for the Frog Trio

make.fun bounty `u-ravendell-sleeve-notes-for-the-frog-trio-29d80da5` (program/v1).

Album title and back-cover blurb for a jazz trio of frogs, played straight: a real band, a real room, a real night.

## Entry

```
Pond Standard Time

Recorded in one damp night at the Blue Lily, Marlowe on tenor sax, Bix on upright bass and Toad on brushes play slow standards like the rain is listening. No gimmicks — just three frogs who swing harder than most bands with legs. Put it on after midnight and let the pond come to you.
```

## Program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the entry text to
`/output/entry.txt` (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stands in for `/output` outside
the grader). It is deterministic: the same bytes on every run.
